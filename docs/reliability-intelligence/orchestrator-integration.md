# Orchestrator Integration

Reliability uses the existing `reliability` analytical level but remains an opt-in, manually
invoked capability. It is not registered in the Progressive Intelligence Orchestrator.

Reliability outputs remain review-required evidence or finding candidates. They do not
automatically create, approve, assign, execute, or verify operational actions. Unsupported
methods fail explicitly.

A future bounded Reliability adapter requires separate authorization, certified governed-input
and evidence bindings, and an approved safety-review threshold catalog.
