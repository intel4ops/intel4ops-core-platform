# Oilfield Job-to-Cash Certification Scenario

`J2C-OILFIELD-001` is a profile of the governed Job-to-Cash pack, not another
platform. It contains two branches, three customers, commercial and field-operational
records, expected and actual billing, recovery, finance verification, and reversal.
Fourteen declared defects challenge missing charges, rate and document errors,
purchase-order exhaustion, duplicate and late events, partial payment, currency safety,
post-recovery credit, and cross-tenant references.
