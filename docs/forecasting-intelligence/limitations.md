# Limitations and deferred work

Monthly and larger periods currently use bounded elapsed-day offsets rather than a full
business-calendar service. Deep hierarchy reconciliation, multiplicative seasonality,
unconstrained multivariate models, unknown future drivers, ARIMA automation, probabilistic and
Bayesian models, external signals, causal claims, online learning, streaming updates,
hyperparameter search, predictive maintenance, and autonomous commitments are deferred.
Intervals are empirical and do not provide calibrated probabilistic guarantees.
