# Forecast readiness

Forecast readiness consumes, and never replaces, the Trust Engine decision. It reports eligible
and ineligible methods, blocking reasons, history, seasonal cycles, imputation rate, partial and
late counts, and unit, currency, and grain status. Missing values, duplicate periods, mixed
measurement contexts, insufficient history, excessive imputation, unsupported methods, and
unapproved partial-period use block execution with structured outcomes.
