# Pump P-204 Example

Intel4Ops retains the reliability assessment that Pump P-204 has an estimated 82% probability of
drive-end bearing failure within 18 days, including confidence, evidence, model version, and
limitations. A linked inspection action recommends a reliability engineer, vibration and
temperature measurements, four labor hours, and bearing `BRG-204`; inventory remains unknown until
confirmed.

The action is prioritized, routed for approval, planned, assigned, scheduled, and executed.
Completion evidence records the inspection, but the action remains unverified. An authorized
verifier reviews post-intervention vibration evidence, verifies the action, and only then records
realized avoided cost separately from the earlier expected value. Structured feedback classifies a
non-failure after intervention as a prevented event rather than proof the prediction was wrong.
