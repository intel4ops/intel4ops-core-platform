# Platform Integrations

- Reliability executions remain immutable prediction sources and receive structured outcome
  feedback only after verified intervention.
- Forecast executions remain historical; uncertainty, horizon, revisions, and limitations are
  referenced rather than rewritten.
- Findings may originate actions without implicit finding status changes.
- Trust limitations lower confidence and can increase deterministic approval requirements.
- Expected recovery remains distinct from verified realized recovery; legacy recovery APIs remain
  backward compatible.
- Intelligence orchestration request references preserve the analytical decision trace.
- Source-system, work-order, inventory, reservation, workforce, permit, and document references are
  ready for future Connect adapters but do not claim external confirmation without evidence.

A prevented event is classified separately from a false positive. WP-2.14 records calibration
feedback but never retrains or deploys a model automatically.
