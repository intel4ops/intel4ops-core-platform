# Statistical Intelligence Limitations

WP-2.11 deliberately defers supervised learning, clustering beyond bounded methods,
neural networks, predictive maintenance, Bayesian/causal models, forecasting,
advanced seasonality decomposition, training pipelines, feature/model stores, online
learning, model monitoring, auto-tuning, learned thresholds, AI-authored methods,
cross-tenant raw benchmarks, autonomous fraud/misconduct/safety conclusions, and
recovery optimization.

Seasonality-adjusted deviation, distribution shift, correlation and reconciliation
are represented as future capability classes but are not executable. Cohort creation
is caller-governed and aggregate-only; a future privacy service should add formal
minimum-cell and differential privacy controls. Execution is synchronous and bounded
to 1,000 observations rather than distributed or streaming.
