# Statistical Explainability

Each observation exposes method/version, selection reason, comparison basis, baseline
summary and fingerprint, sample size, time window, privacy-safe peer summary, observed
and expected values, absolute/relative/normalized deviation, threshold, statistical
score, confidence, materiality, severity, persistence, recurrence, limitations,
alternative explanations, and recommended verification.

Evidence references connect the execution to dataset and lineage fingerprints, Trust
and readiness decisions, OIKB version and package fingerprint, method trace, baseline,
and scoring components. Raw operational rows are not duplicated in the evidence
payload.
