# Statistical Baselines

WP-2.11 persists reproducible aggregate summaries, never copied raw source rows.
Baseline records capture tenant scope, population inclusion/exclusion, time window,
record/entity counts, central tendency, dispersion, percentiles, limitations, and a
fingerprint over immutable inputs.

Historical-self, rolling-window, organization and aggregate-only peer baselines are
executable when supported by the selected method and canonical request. Other declared
baseline types remain governed vocabulary until corresponding canonical cohort
services exist. Peer results disclose no other tenant's observations and require an
explicit minimum cohort.
